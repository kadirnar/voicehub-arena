Frozen protocol

English only · seed 42 · one generation per target. Corpus WER/CER aggregate edit counts. Whisper-large-v3 (Systran conversion, revision edaa852ec7e145841d8ffdb056a99866b5f0a478), CUDA FP16, beam 5, temperature 0, no VAD, no previous-text conditioning; whisper_english normalization. Full-run confidence intervals use 1,000 prompt bootstrap resamples.

Llasa: independent Transformers LM/tokenizer, BF16, temperature 0.8, top-p 1, top-k 50; frozen independently audited XCodec2 codec in FP32. Dia: independent Transformers processor, LM and DAC in FP32. Dia2: official Nari Labs implementation, BF16, CFG 2, audio temperature 0.8 and top-k 50; fixed prefix word alignment is prepared once with Whisper-large-v3. Reference audio is excluded from scored output.

The active Llasa selection is HKUSTAudio/Llasa-1B, HKUSTAudio/Llasa-3B and HKUSTAudio/Llasa-8B. Multilingual, Preserve-TextChat and multi-speaker variants have been removed from the active evaluation. Dia and both Dia2 checkpoints remain selected; every checkpoint is pinned to a commit. Community fine-tunes and duplicate quantizations are outside this inventory. Fixed reference speech differs from the official per-sample speaker-similarity protocol.
