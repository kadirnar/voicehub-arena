Measurement protocol

English only. Models run serially in separate processes. Warm-up is excluded from latency; CUDA is synchronized. Public evaluations use pinned Whisper large-v3 (CTranslate2 FP16 on CUDA), beam size 5, no VAD, and no reference-text prompt. ASR starts after synthesis releases the GPU. Historical runs retain their recorded scorer.

WER and CER reflect both synthesis and recognition errors. The public protocol uses whisper-normalizer 0.1.12 EnglishTextNormalizer; raw and orthographic scores are also retained. Corpus error rates are weighted by total reference units, with separate utterance averages. Repeated seeds are separate generations. Compare matching sample coverage and settings. Scores from different datasets or coverage stages are never pooled. Public test texts use each provider’s fixed voice or fixed reference: this measures intelligibility, not the official Seed zero-shot SIM or Emergent model-as-judge score. LibriTTS and LibriSpeech share source material. WER/CER cannot measure emotion, prosody, or naturalness. Partial coverage is ineligible for ranking.

RTF is generation seconds per audio second. GPU peak is PyTorch allocated memory, not total device memory. Silence is the proportion of samples in 20 ms frames below −40 dBFS RMS. Signal metrics are diagnostics, not human quality ratings. TTFA requires a streaming adapter. Human MOS, speaker similarity, PESQ and STOI are not estimated without their required ratings or paired references.

The latest view uses each model’s newest attempt with the same dataset, repeats and ASR settings. Pending retries replace old failure labels. Completed means measured; inspect transcripts and quality flags before judging quality. Previous runs remain available above.
