Measurement protocol

English only. Models run serially in separate processes. Warm-up is excluded from latency; CUDA is synchronized. ASR uses a pinned faster-whisper checkpoint on CPU, beam size 5, no VAD, and no reference-text prompt.

WER and CER reflect both synthesis and recognition errors. Lowercase and punctuation normalization is applied; corpus error rates are weighted by total reference units. Repeated seeds are separate generations. Compare matching sample coverage and settings. This short diagnostic set is not a publication-grade ranking.

RTF is generation seconds per audio second. GPU peak is PyTorch allocated memory, not total device memory. Silence is the proportion of samples in 20 ms frames below −40 dBFS RMS. Signal metrics are diagnostics, not human quality ratings. TTFA requires a streaming adapter. Human MOS, speaker similarity, PESQ and STOI are not estimated without their required ratings or paired references.
